Tap here to change the Arabic script: Amiri, IndoPak or Uthmani.

About

The morning and evening adhkār, and Sūrat al-Kahf, one page at a time: Arabic first, then English.

Watch the how-to video

How to use

  • Swipe to move between pages. On a computer, use the side buttons or the arrow keys.
  • Before midday the morning adhkār open, and after midday the evening adhkār. Tap the title to switch.
  • Use the menu at the top to switch between the adhkār and Sūrat al-Kahf.
  • Tap ▶ Listen to hear a dhikr or a page of Sūrat al-Kahf recited. In Sūrat al-Kahf, tap a verse number to hear that verse.
  • Tap the font name at the top of an Arabic page to change the script.
  • Use − and + to change the text size, and the moon or sun to switch between dark and light.
  • Your place, font, text size and theme are remembered on this device.

Fonts and languages

  • Amiri: a classic Naskh script, clear and easy to read.
  • IndoPak: the Nastaʿlīq-style script of mushafs from South Asia, with its own pause marks.
  • Uthmani: the King Fahd Complex script used in the Madinah mushaf.
  • English: the adhkār in UWT's translation; Āyat al-Kursī, the three Quls and Sūrat al-Kahf in the translation by Aisha and Abdalhaqq Bewley.

Add it to your home screen

Install it like an app: it opens full screen from its own icon and works without a connection.

  • iPhone or iPad: open this page in Safari, tap the Share button (the square with an arrow; on newer iOS it may be inside the ⋯ menu), then Add to Home Screen.
  • Android: open this page in Chrome, tap the ⋮ menu, then Add to Home screen or Install app.
  • Computer: in Chrome or Edge, click the install icon at the right of the address bar.
